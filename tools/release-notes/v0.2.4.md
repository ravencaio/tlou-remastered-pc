**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.2.4

Volumetric light without dark wedges or broken sun rays, a mode for 2-3 GB graphics cards, and
launcher programs that antivirus software should mistake less often for malware.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

**Discord:** help, testing and news: https://discord.gg/9mnjjezcNj

## Fixes and improvements

- **Volumetric light** ([#55](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/55)): three
  faults in how the game's light-shaft shaders were translated for the PC.
  - **Dark or bright wedges through a light** that swept across the screen as the camera moved
    (seen at night in the suburbs, on any NVIDIA card) are gone. Those shaders pick values from
    arrays kept in the GPU's registers: the arrays lost their top elements, and on NVIDIA the last
    lanes of each group of 32 were never served, so a block of the light's sampling lines missed
    the light.
  - **Sun rays on RTX cards** with hard edges and bright streaks pointing at the sun (clearest in
    photo mode at the start of Pittsburgh): the shader that averages them now synchronizes its
    threads as the PS4 does. GTX 10 series cards got it right by luck; newer cards did not. Not yet
    tested on an RTX card: please tell us if the rays still look wrong there.
  - The light can still look a little odd right at the edges of objects in front of it. That may be
    the game's own technique; a PS4 recording of the same spot would settle it.
- **Video memory for 2-3 GB cards**: the launcher's new **Video memory** choice (**Automatic**,
  **2-3 GB (saving mode)**, **4 GB or more**). The saving mode uses smaller reserves, drops old
  textures sooner and, when the card is full, moves data to system memory instead of stopping the
  game. On a GTX 1050 Ti at 1080p the game went from about 2.4 GB of video memory to about 1.6 GB
  at the same frame rate. **Automatic** turns it on for cards of up to 3 GB. Pair it with 1280x720
  or 1600x900 (or an upscaler). Not yet tested on a real 2 GB card.
- **Antivirus false positives** ([#148](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/148)):
  Windows Defender's cloud scan and a few engines on VirusTotal flagged the package. The two
  starter programs (**TLOU Launcher.exe**, **TLOU Game.exe**) now carry version information and a
  manifest, are much smaller, and no longer start the launcher through a hidden command prompt.
  Whether every detection goes away is only known once the new package is scanned.
- For testers of the wall stripes on RTX cards
  ([#58](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/58)): `GOW3_MANUAL_INTERP=0` lets an
  RTX card interpolate the way GTX cards do, to compare.

The first start after updating compiles the compute shaders again once.

## Update

**From 0.1.8 or newer:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:** extract the
zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis` folder.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

## Known issues

- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- Stripes on some walls on RTX cards ([#58](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/58)).
- A thin light ring can remain where wheels and feet touch the ground: the game's own design,
  made thinner by **Contact shadows at half resolution**.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.
- Dubs from other regions have not been tried with files from a European or Japanese copy yet.
- Online play (Factions) is not available.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher), or ask
on the Discord server.
